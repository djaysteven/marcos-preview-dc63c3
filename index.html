01 — Our story

From a market stall in Jomtien to Thappraya Road.

Marco — Italian and French roots — opened in 2013 at Jomtien's Rompho market. Today Marco's seats around a hundred guests on Thappraya Road, with a visible pizza preparation area so you can watch yours being made.

Much of what's on your plate comes from Italy. The beef is Australian; free-range chicken and organic eggs come from Klong Phai farm. The walk-in wine cellar holds more than 110 labels, 17 of them by the glass — Italy first, then France, Spain, Argentina and beyond.
